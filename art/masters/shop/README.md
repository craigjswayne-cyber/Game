## The sawtooth held to schedule

This facility's signature is a sawtooth north-light roof: parallel bays, each a pale opaque south
slope and a band of north-facing angled glazing, reading from overhead as regular alternating
stripes of metal and glass. It is unique to this building in the campus.

It was specified for level 4 and forbidden by name in the level 1, 2 and 3 prompts. **It appears at
level 4 and not before** — level 3 is a plain hipped roof with no trace of it — and carries into
level 5 with solar panels replacing the glazing on the eastern third of the bays.

That is the second facility running where naming the signature in earlier levels' DO NOT ADD lists
held it to its intended level. Together with the centre of excellence, this is now a reliable
control.

## On why the palette held here, honestly

This run used the same three pins as Hospitality & Boxes, which was generated immediately before it
and broke at both level 4 and level 5, its level 5 landing at `9,91,33`. Same wording, same numbers,
opposite outcome.

**The reason is not established.** The most plausible difference is that a retail shed is a less
grand brief than a double-tiered executive box complex, so there is less pulling the render toward a
showpiece — but that is a guess, not a finding.

The honest conclusion across all ten facilities: **the pins reduce the failure rate, they do not
eliminate it.** Budget a correction pass for level 5, and for level 4 on any facility whose level 4
is a large step up. When the first attempt comes back clean, as here, take it.

## Site contract

Hedge, 6 m perimeter path, four mid-edge connector stubs, bollards, wider south stub with threshold
slab, four corner trees. Central mass growing outward behind a 6 m apron ring the stubs run in to
meet. Sun high to the north-west, every shadow down and to the right. No text, no people, no
vehicles, and no signage or branding of any kind, which this facility was most at risk of.
