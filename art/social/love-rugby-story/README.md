# Love rugby? Instagram Story ad

`phase-love-rugby-story-1080x1920.png` is the 9:16 Story. `safe-zone-check.png` is the
same image with Instagram's covered areas marked: the top 270 px, the bottom 380 px
and the 65 px side margins.

`node art/social/love-rugby-story/render.cjs` re-renders both from `story.html` and
prints whether each element (headline, subline, phone, logo, free line) sits inside
the safe band. `screen.png` is a real capture of the game's home screen.
