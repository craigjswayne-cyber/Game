# Press release: PHASE: Rugby Manager 1.9.10

Written 4 Nov 2026. Covers 1.9.7 to 1.9.10. Gender neutral on purpose: every
change below is in both the men's and the women's game, and the text says
"players" and "they" throughout. Keep it that way in any edit.

---

**PHASE: Rugby Manager 1.9.10 is out on Google Play**

*Four updates in a row, built from player feedback: players who learn new
positions, a training load with a real trade-off, and a transfer search that
finds exactly who you need. In the men's and the women's game, in six
languages.*

FWDS & BCKS LTD today released version 1.9.10 of PHASE: Rugby Manager, the
rugby union management game for Android. It completes a run of four updates
(1.9.7 to 1.9.10) shaped almost entirely by reports from the game's players
and its Discord community.

**Learning a new position**
Players can now retrain in a new position. Sometimes they come to the
manager's office and ask; sometimes the manager decides. A move to a
neighbouring position usually works. A forward becoming a back, or the other
way round, rarely does. Age, attributes, the assistant coach and whether the
player asked all shift the odds, and the coaches say how likely it is before
it starts. It takes a personal-plan place for 8 to 16 weeks, and the inbox
reports whether it worked.

**Training with a cost**
A new training load: Light, Normal or Hard. Hard training improves players
faster but costs fitness and brings more training-ground injuries. Light
training gives the legs back. Each week the Training screen shows what the
last one achieved.

**Finding the right player**
The transfer search can now filter on several attributes at once against one
minimum, out of 100, the same scale as the player page. Each player's
Attributes tab shows their rating at every position they can play. Free
agents and players on the loan list can now be opened, shortlisted and
scouted.

**Feedback fixed**
- Wage room no longer runs out over a long career, and existing careers get
  theirs back when they load.
- Back returns to a list with its filters, tab and scroll position kept.
- Team > General Info shows each player's rating and fits small phones.
- The fatigue warning on Tactics now comes from the club's physio, by name.

**Two games, one standard**
PHASE has a men's game and a women's game, kept fully separate. Every change
in 1.9 was tested in both. French, Spanish and Italian text was checked line
by line so a women's career reads correctly in each.

**Available now**
PHASE: Rugby Manager is free on Google Play, with an optional Pro Manager
upgrade. Available in English, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese and
Afrikaans.
