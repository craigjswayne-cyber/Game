# PHASE: Rugby Manager 2.0.0: deep dive, improvements and art list

Written 21 Nov 2026 at the owner's request: "The game needs upgrades to keep it
interesting. I want this new in-game mode with stadiums, sponsors etc. Deep dive
into the game, suggest improvements and custom designs I should get to elevate
the game."

Everything said about the game below comes from a file that was opened for this
review, and the path is given. Nothing here changes code. Sizes: **small** is a
day or two of work, **medium** is about a week, **large** is several weeks or a
big batch of art.

## 2. The game as it stands

### 2.1 Screens

Routing is in `src/ui/App.tsx` (the `switch` on `cur.screen`) and `src/store/nav.ts`.
Screens live in `src/ui/screens/`:

- **Career and week:** Menu, NewGame, Home (the Today desk, `src/game/desk.ts`), DayRoom (the week day by day, `src/game/days.ts`), Inbox, Wire (league gossip), Press, Profile, Jobs, Saves, Settings, Handbook, About, BugReport, Supporter (the store).
- **Squad:** Squad, PlayerScreen, Selection, DepthChart, Training, DevelopmentPanel, MentoringPanel, Medical, Academy, TeamReport, DreamTeam.
- **Match:** MatchDay, then `screens/matchday/` (Preview, NationPreview, Live, Panels, Touchline, Stats, Ratings, ScoreCard, SquadSheet, Verdict), WeekResults, Fixtures, Tables, SeasonStats.
- **Club:** ClubScreen, Finances (with Commercial and The Board tabs), Infrastructure (the campus map, `src/ui/CampusMap.tsx`), Transfers, Scouting, Offers, Agency.
- **Long game:** History, Legacy, Annual, SeasonReview, Nations, Country (the national job), DrawRoom.

### 2.2 Systems in `src/game`

The game is unusually deep. Some of what exists, by area:

- **Squad and training:** weekly training, personal plans and recovery (`season/training.ts`), development projects (`devproject.ts`), mentoring (`mentoring.ts`), retraining positions (`retrain.ts`), ageing split into body and craft (`ageing.ts`), condition (`condition.ts`), physio reports (`physio.ts`), playing through a knock (`knock.ts`), squad bonds and cliques (`bonds.ts`), dressing-room authority and culture (`authority.ts`, `culture.ts`, `room.ts`).
- **Transfers and contracts:** AI market and rival bids (`ai.ts`, `rivalbids.ts`), loans (`loans.ts`), scouting and commissioned scouting (`scout.ts`, `commission.ts`, `recruit.ts`), handshake promises (`handshake.ts`), releases (`release.ts`), medical joker signings (`joker.ts`), salary cap and marquee players (`cap.ts`), a league full of people who want things (`living.ts`).
- **Finances and sponsors:** the commercial department with shirt, sleeve, naming and kit slots (`commercial.ts`), sponsor negotiations (`sponsortalks.ts`), the season's books (`books.ts`), upkeep and wear stories (`upkeep.ts`), close-season events at the ground (`closeseason.ts`), administration (`insolvency.ts`), the other clubs' economies (`aiecon.ts`).
- **Club and stadium:** nine facilities at levels 0 to 5 (`model/facilities.ts`, `FACILITY_INFO`), and a separate six-step ground ladder by seats: 1,500, 3,000, 6,000, 9,000, 15,000, 32,000 (`GROUND_TIERS`, `groundLevel`). The board signs off one stand a season if the ground is 90% full (`season/board.ts`, `expansionPlan`, `requestExpansion`), and a following grows only by filling the ground (`demandCeiling`).
- **Fans and press:** fan mood with causes (`fans.ts`), terrace reactions to signings and sales (`terraces.ts`), press barometer (`pressmood.ts`), board memos and the boardroom door (`boardmemo.ts`, `boardroom.ts`), chairman types (`chairman.ts`).
- **Matches:** the engine (`matchEngine.ts`, `match/`), weather and pitch surface (`conditions.ts`: Dry, Damp, Rain, Wind, Snow; grass, hybrid, artificial, researched per real ground), travel and home edge (`venue.ts`), referees (`match/referee.ts`), derbies (`rivalries.ts`) and earned grudges (`grudges.ts`), playbook and moves (`playbook.ts`, `moves.ts`, `armsrace.ts`), opposition coaches who adapt (`oppcoach.ts`, `rematch.ts`).
- **Career and story:** the Dream and ambitions (`dream.ts`, `ambitions.ts`), the career arc and rival coaches remembered as people (`arc.ts`, `arcbook.ts`, `rivalcoach.ts`), the story of an era told every fifth season (`erastory.ts`), records, legends and testimonials (`records.ts`, `legends.ts`, `match/finalize.ts`), the consequence memory (`memory.ts`), board objectives (`objectives.ts`), eight scripted challenges (`newgame.ts`, `CHALLENGES`).
- **Internationals:** the country desk and call-ups (`country.ts`), Test windows (`season/internationals.ts`), world rankings (`natrank.ts`), a World Championship every fourth men's season (`schedule.ts`), the secret Isles XV job (`isles.ts`).
- **Money:** Pro Manager, cash injections, the Estate, full fitness, the international start and a tip jar (`monetise.ts`, `SELLABLE_SKUS`, eleven products), eight opt-in rewarded ads (`rewarded.ts`, `docs/monetisation-spec.md` section 2), and ten banner places on eight screens, one of them under the live match (`<AdSlot>` in Home, Press, WeekResults, Finances, DayRoom, Fixtures, Inbox, Live).

### 2.4 How the live pitch uses the stadium pictures

This matters for every stadium asset, so it is set out exactly:

- On a phone in Extended the pitch is a 4:3 panel (`src/ui/styles/13-live.css`, `.live-wrap.full-match .live-pitch`). A box that is not clearly taller than wide uses the painted picture (`LivePitch.tsx`, `size()`, `up = h > w * 1.25`). A tall box (some tablet layouts) falls back to a ground drawn in code (`paintField`).
- The picture is chosen by `pickGround`: night or day, rain or snow, otherwise whichever of four seat colours (navy, maroon, red, green) is nearest the home kit. It is the same building for every club.
- Each picture has its pitch measured by hand: `x0` (left try line) about 280, `y0` (far touchline) 318, `mx` about 8.87 pixels a metre along, `my` about 7.17 across (`GROUNDS`). So the field runs from x 280 to 1167 and y 318 to 820, with 8 m in-goals (`GOAL = 8`) taking it from x 209 to 1238.
- Only the box x 100 to 1348, y 75 to 1011 is shown (`crop` in `size()`). Anything outside it is never seen.
- The advertising boards in the painted pictures are plain colour bands. Measured on `stadium-snow.webp`: far side two runs, x 202 to 527 and 923 to 1246, at y 275 to 290; near side one run, x 202 to 1246, at y 848 to 866.
- **The sponsor names are only drawn in the code-drawn fallback** (`paintField`, `nameAt`). In the painted view on a phone, which is the main view, no sponsor is shown. `Live.tsx` (`pitchBoards`) already passes the right names in: the user's own deals first when at home, then the invented brands.
- On a phone the picture is drawn at about a third of its size (`dim.art = min(w / 1248, h / 936)`, so a 390-point-wide panel gives 0.31). A 15-pixel board is about 5 points tall on screen. Words on boards will not read; a bold logo in two colours will.

## 3. The judgement

### 3.1 Where the hook is strong

Under the surface this is the deepest rugby manager on a phone. The career
remembers (`memory.ts`, `arcbook.ts`), rival coaches carry a record against you
(`rivalcoach.ts`), the dressing room has factions (`bonds.ts`, `culture.ts`),
the economy is balanced against probes so money means something
(`commercial.ts` on mean-neutrality), and there is a secret top job
(`isles.ts`). Football Manager players will recognise the depth. My read is that few
rugby managers on phones go this deep; the gap is in how it looks, not in
what it does.

### 3.2 Where a save goes flat

- **Season 2.** Every week looks the same as last season's. The ground has grown on the campus map but not on match day, and the live pitch is the same stadium for a village club and for a 25,000-seat club. The sponsor deals renewed in the summer are not visible anywhere you watch.
- **Season 5.** The facility ladder is near its top (or bought outright with the Estate, `grants.ts`, `applyEstate`), the ground has hit its catchment (`demandCeiling`), and the same ten sponsor names per slot come round again. The things that pile up over a long save (trophies, records, legends) are shown as lists with one icon. Out of the Park Baseball and Football Manager keep players for decades with exactly those piles: a cabinet you can look at, a wall of names, a ground with history on it.
- **Shallow or missing for rugby fans:** nations have no colours of their own on the pitch (`kit.ts`), no pre-match rituals, kits drawn without their patterns, the surface researched per real ground (`conditions.ts`, `PITCH`) but always drawn as striped grass, wind and damp not shown, and night chosen by a hash because the game has no kick-off times (`Live.tsx`, comment on `pitchNight`).
- **Interface:** dated where it is all rows and chips. Motorsport Manager is the model to copy: the same amount of information, but every screen has one picture that tells you where you are (the garage, the HQ, the paddock). One background or header picture per area would change how the game feels without touching a system.

### 3.3 Stadiums and sponsors: what the data already gives you

| Data | Where | What the match view could do with it |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity and ground tier 0 to 5 | `Club.capacity`, `groundLevel` | Pick the stadium picture for that tier |
| A stand being built | `state.stadiumBuild` (`season/board.ts`) | Scaffolding and a crane on one stand |
| Gate for this match | `fx.att`, set before kick-off in `match/begin.ts` | Full, three-quarters or half-full stands |
| Derby, knockout, final venue | `fx.derby`, `fx.stage`, `fx.venue`, `finalVenue` (`model/competition.ts`, seven neutral grounds) | Big-match dressing, a national stadium for finals |
| Playing-surface facility level | `facilities.pitch` | Pitch quality and wear |
| Surface type | `conditions.ts`, `PITCH` | Artificial looks flatter, no mow stripes |
| Weather | `fixture.weather` (`Dry`, `Damp`, `Rain`, `Wind`, `Snow`) | Today only Rain and Snow reach the pitch |
| Hospitality level | `facilities.hospitality` | Glass boxes in the main stand at higher tiers |
| Sponsors per slot | `state.deals`, `commercial.ts` `SLOTS` and `NAMES` | Boards, halfway logo, kit icon |

| Club colours and kit pattern | `Club.colors`, `kits.ts` `KIT_PATTERNS` | Seats, discs with hoops, tunnel canopy |
| Fan mood | `state.fanMood` (`fans.ts`) | Banners for or against the manager |

## 4. Improvements by area

Each item: what it is, what it builds on, size, and the art it needs (asset
codes refer to section 5).

### 4.1 Match day

1. **Stadium by ground tier.** Extend `GROUNDS` so the key is tier plus condition, and choose with `groundLevel(club.capacity)` for the home side, or the national stadium when `fx.venue` is set. Keep `pickGround`'s weather logic. Builds on `LivePitch.tsx` (`GROUNDS`, `pickGround`), `model/facilities.ts`. Medium code. Art: S1 to S6, S9.
2. **Sponsor boards in the painted view.** In `draw()`, when `dim.art` is set, draw each board as a filled rectangle at the measured board rectangles (section 2.4) with a sponsor logo fitted to its height. Store the board rectangles per picture next to `x0`, `y0`. Builds on `pitchBoards` (`Live.tsx`) and `nameAt` (`LivePitch.tsx`). Small to medium. Art: B1, B2, L1 to L40.
3. **Halfway and in-goal logos.** Draw the club crest (or the competition mark in a final) at halfway between the 10 m lines, about 177 x 177 source pixels centred on x 724, y 569, at about 35% opacity under the players. Sponsor wordmarks in the in-goals (x 209 to 280 and 1167 to 1238), turned to read from the side. Builds on `Crest` (`components.tsx`), `SX`/`SY` in `LivePitch.tsx`. Small. Art: C1 to C21 for finals; L-set for in-goals.
4. **Crowd from the gate.** Ratio `fx.att / capacity` decides how many seat blocks show the full crowd; the rest show empty seats. Needs a matching empty-seat picture per tier and a list of seat-block rectangles per picture. Medium. Art: S8.
5. **Big-match dressing.** When `fx.stage` is a final, when `fx.derby`, in a Test, or when the gate is a club record (the record-gate check in `season/finance.ts`), lay a transparent dressing picture over the stadium. Builds on `match/begin.ts` (`big`, `derby`). Small code, art: D1 to D4.
6. **Weather and time of day.** Damp gets a light wet sheen, Wind gets flags moving on the stand roofs, fog for cold damp mornings; replace the night hash with kick-off times by competition and month (Friday night league games, afternoon cup ties, winter Tests at dusk), with a warm dusk colour wash in code over the day picture. Builds on `conditions.ts`, `Live.tsx` `pitchNight`. Medium. Art: W1 to W4.
7. **Pitch surface and wear.** Artificial pitches drawn flatter with no stripes (a code tint), wear that grows over the season and with a low playing-surface level, mud when wet. Builds on `conditions.ts` `PITCH`, `facilities.pitch`. Medium. Art: P1 to P6.
8. **Kit patterns on discs.** Clip a hoop, stripe, quarter or sash pattern inside each disc in the second colour. Builds on `kits.ts` (`KIT_PATTERNS`) and the disc drawing in `LivePitch.tsx`. Small. No art.
9. **More highlight kinds** (already on the owner's list): line breaks, try-saving tackles, turnovers, driving mauls, long kick chases, interception tries. Builds on `src/ui/highlight/build.ts` and the new engine's record (`docs/match-sim-spec.md` section 2). Medium once the engine lands. No art.
10. **Derby flashpoints** (owner's 1.9.16 note): an occasional scuffle shown as a huddle of discs and a card, under a strict referee only. Builds on `match/discipline.ts`, `match/referee.ts`. Small. No art.

### 4.2 Squad and training

1. **Rotation planner** (roadmap section 3): mark players to rest, the assistant respects it. Builds on `Selection.tsx`, `condition.ts`, `gametime.ts`. Small. No art.
2. **Training ground move preview** (ideas list): the Moves screen plays the move on a training pitch with cones. Builds on `MovePreview.tsx`, `tacticsArt.tsx`. Medium. Art: reuse `campus/training-pitch` style; one top-down training pitch picture (in group X, X5).
3. **Height and weight** (working list 1): only if the new engine wants power and pace; it touches every save and balance probe. Large. No art.
4. **Training screen header picture.** A training-ground background behind the week's plan. Small. Art: X4.

2. **Signing unveil card:** a big signing gets a card at the ground in your kit colours (no face), which the terraces story already celebrates in words (`terraces.ts`). Small. Art: M8.
3. **Free agents at the start and a free-agent filter** (owner's 1.9.16 notes). Builds on `Transfers.tsx`, `ai.ts`. Small. No art.

### 4.4 Club and stadium

1. **Ground tiers visible everywhere.** The same tier picture (cropped) as the header of ClubScreen and Infrastructure, so the ground you are building is the ground you play in. Builds on `ClubScreen.tsx`, `Infrastructure.tsx`, `CampusMap.tsx`. Small once S-art exists.
2. **Construction you can watch.** While `stadiumBuild` is on, the match picture shows a stand under scaffolding. Builds on `season/board.ts`. Small code. Art: S7.

### 4.5 Finances and sponsors

2. **Sponsor tiers by ground.** Split each slot's names into local, regional and national lists; the offers come from the list that matches the ground tier and reputation. Moving up a tier brings a visibly bigger brand. Builds on `offersFor`, `NAMES`. Small code, art L-set grows (the 40 listed are the current names; add 20 more later).
3. **Naming rights you can read.** The sponsored ground name on the scoreboard or main stand roof in the match view. Builds on `Club.stadium`, `stadiumBase`. Small.
4. **Sponsor days.** A sponsor's match: their logo at halfway, a branded matchday programme card, a bonus for a win. Builds on `sponsortalks.ts` (performance terms), `closeseason.ts`. Small to medium.

### 4.6 Career and story

1. **Honours badges.** A wall of badges earned across a career (first win, first trophy, promotion, a derby double, an unbeaten home season, a record gate, a stand opened, an academy graduate capped, ten seasons at one club, a Test series won). The game has objectives and challenges but no badges (`objectives.ts`, `newgame.ts` `CHALLENGES`; searching for an achievement system found only the inbox note in `model/types.ts`). Medium. Art: H1 to H24.
2. **A real trophy cabinet.** Trophy art per competition on Legacy and SeasonReview, and a trophy-lift card. Small code. Art: T1 to T12, M1.
3. **Moment cards for the career's big days:** promotion, relegation, title, sacking, new job, international call, testimonial, new stand opened. Builds on `SeasonReview.tsx`, `Annual.tsx`, `erastory.ts`. Small code. Art: M1 to M12.
4. **Rival managers as people:** a silhouette and a short line on the preview when you meet a coach with history. Builds on `rivalcoach.ts`, `boss.ts`. Small. No art (silhouette in code).

### 4.7 Internationals

1. **National colours.** A small table of each nation's two colours in `nations.ts`, read by `kitColours` instead of `--kit-home`/`--kit-away`. Colours are not a trademark. Small. No art.
2. **Pre-match ritual formation** (owner's 1.9.16 note): for nations that have one, the side lines up in a block before kick-off. Builds on `livepitch/shapes.ts`. Small. No art.
3. **The national stadium.** Tests at home and the seven neutral finals grounds (`EURO_FINAL_VENUES`) use one big-bowl picture with Test dressing. Small code. Art: S9, D3.
4. **Tension in close Tests** (owner's 1.9.16 decision): already decided; the dressing and the crowd lines support it.
5. **A women's World Championship** (working list 6). One to two weeks per that note. No art beyond a trophy (T8 serves both).

### 4.8 Interface and feel

1. **One picture per area.** A dark, moody background or header per area in the style of `title-bg.webp`: boardroom, press room, physio room, training ground, analysis room, dressing room, tunnel, trophy room, federation office, transfer office, academy. Small code each. Art: X1 to X11.
2. **Crest emblems.** Crests at 48 px and above carry an emblem instead of only letters, from a library of invented symbols assigned by hash. Builds on `Crest` (`components.tsx`). Small. Art: E1 to E24.
3. **Cut text on the briefing and preview.** The owner flagged the briefing. Lead with three lines and a picture (the opposition's kit, the ground, the weather icon), put the rest behind "more". Builds on `matchday/Preview.tsx`. Small. No art.

### 4.9 Onboarding

1. **Show the live pitch in the first friendly.** Either default Extended or a one-tap choice before the first kick-off. Builds on `matchPrefs.ts`, `firststeps.ts`. Small.
2. **Add a sixth first step: visit your ground.** The campus map and the ground tier picture, so a new manager knows the estate exists. Builds on `firststeps.ts` (`StepId`). Small.
3. **Point at Offers and the Store once.** Both feedback reports show Offers never opened. A single nudge in the first week, nothing repeated. Small.

### 4.10 Money without annoying players

1. **Keep the ground tiers earned.** The Estate does not touch capacity (`applyEstate` sets facility levels only), so stadium tiers stay something you earn. Keep it that way: never sell a tier, a gate boost or a following.
2. **Cosmetics for Pro Manager.** Pro already gives skins and save slots (`docs/pro-manager.md` section 1). Add match-day cosmetics that change nothing in the simulation: choice of seat colour family, a custom flag in the stands, the night or dusk look for every home match. Monetisation rule 2 in `docs/monetisation.md` stays true.
3. **A rewarded "sponsor's matchday"** only if a ninth placement is wanted: watch an advert, the sponsor dresses a friendly. Cosmetic only. Small.
4. **Leave the live match free of new adverts.** There is already a banner under the live match (`Live.tsx`, `match-foot`). Do not add one over the pitch or between halves.

## 5. Custom design list for the image generator

### 5.1 How to use it

- **Style of the existing stadium pictures** (looked at: `stadium-green`, `stadium-night-wet`, `stadium-snow`): painted, semi-realistic, clean, like a premium mobile game; seen from almost straight above with the view squashed a little across the pitch (it measures 8.87 pixels a metre along and 7.17 across); pitch landscape, try lines left and right, home tunnel and two glass dugouts with red seats on the far (top) touchline; long covered stands top and bottom with pale translucent roof panels in bays, smaller covered stands at each end, crowd as fine dots of colour; four floodlight masts on the grass at the corners; clipped hedges and round trees outside; deep saturated green grass mown in vertical stripes, white markings, red corner flags, white posts with red pads. Day: soft even light from the top, light shadows falling down and to the right. Night: dark navy surround, lamps glowing, the centre of the pitch brighter. Snow: surround, roofs and trees frosted white, the pitch itself clear green.
- **Always attach an existing picture as the reference** (`stadium-green.webp` for day, `stadium-night.webp` for night) and say the pitch must not move. Image models will not hold exact pixels from words alone; the facility work found the same (`art/masters/README.md`).
- **After each stadium picture,** resize to exactly 1448 x 1086, measure `x0`, `y0`, `mx`, `my` and the board rectangles as was done for the twelve (comments on `GROUNDS` in `LivePitch.tsx`), and add them to the table. Keep everything that matters inside x 100 to 1348, y 75 to 1011.
- **Transparent backgrounds:** image models usually return an opaque picture. For anything marked "transparent", ask for a flat pure black (or pure magenta #FF00FF for dark subjects) background and cut it out, then save as WebP with transparency. For soft effects (fog, wear, rain) ask for **white on black** and let the code use brightness as transparency: that keeps soft edges clean.
- **No words in any picture.** The game is in six languages and every name changes; code draws all text. The only exception is a sponsor wordmark, which is the brand itself.
- **Formats:** ship WebP, as the game does (`scripts/art/to_webp.py`). Keep stadium pictures near the current 214 to 337 KB each; 49 more stadium pictures is roughly another 13 MB, so check the app size before adding the P2 rows.
- **Priority:** P1 is for 2.0.0, P2 the release after, P3 later.

## 6. What not to do

- **Real club names, crests, kits with sponsor marks, or real sponsor brands** on boards, shirts or anywhere else. The game's own rule is "Sponsor marks always go" (`docs/ip-rename-map.md`, rule 2). The invented names in `commercial.ts` need a quick search each before they become logos: a logo makes a name look like a brand, and a name like "Voltura" or "Trueline" may already belong to someone in sportswear or energy.
- **Crest emblems or competition marks that echo real ones:** no red rose, thistle, shamrock, silver fern, springbok, cockerel, three feathers, kiwi, wallaby, tiger, saint's halo, harlequin diamonds, crescent, shark or anything close to a real club's badge. Assign emblems by hash, not by club, so nobody's real symbol lands on their club.
- **Faces of real players.** The game uses real names; adding generated likenesses of real people is a much bigger image-rights risk. Moment cards use backs and silhouettes.
- **Recognisable real stadiums.** The finals grounds are already renamed (`model/competition.ts`, `EURO_FINAL_VENUES`); the art must be generic too.
- **A bespoke stadium for every club.** Around 170 grounds times six conditions is over a thousand pictures, and the facility work showed the style drifts across even six levels (`art/masters/README.md`). Six tiers plus a national stadium is the right size.
- **Seat colour times tier times weather.** Four seat colours across every tier and condition multiplies the stadium list by four. Club colour belongs in code.
- **Words baked into pictures.** Six languages, and names change every season.
- **Selling stadium tiers, gate boosts or a bigger following.** That turns the best long-term hook into a shop item. The store already sells cash and a maxed estate (`grants.ts`); do not add more power to it.
- **More adverts in the match.** There is one banner under the live match already (`Live.tsx`, `match-foot`).
- **A moving camera or 3D.** The owner ruled out the tracking camera (`LivePitch.tsx` header comment) and battery on an older phone is still unmeasured (`docs/release-notes-2.0.0.md`).
- **Starting the stadium code before the new engine is settled** is fine for art, but any change to how the pitch draws should wait for the engine (`docs/match-sim-spec.md`, "Hold until new engine").
