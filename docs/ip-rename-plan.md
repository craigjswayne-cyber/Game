# v1.0.2 IP rename plan

Decisions taken: **club names become the city**, **leagues become generic-descriptive**,
**grounds become location-based**, and **player names are unchanged**.

Nothing here is applied yet. This file is the proposal for sign-off.

## 1. Competitions

| id | now | proposed | why |
|----|-----|----------|-----|
| `prem` | Gallagher Premiership | **English Premier Division** | drops the sponsor entirely and says what it is |
| `champ` | English Championship | **English Second Division** | "Championship" is the RFU mark; the tier is not |
| `natl1` | National League One | **English National Division** | same shape, not the registered competition |
| `top14` | Top 14 | **French Elite 14** | keeps the 14 because the club count is a fact, not a brand |
| `prod2` | Pro D2 | **French Second Division** | "Pro D2" is an LNR mark |
| `urc` | United Rugby Championship | **United Provinces League** | they are largely provinces; drops the registered title |
| `srp` | Super Rugby Pacific | **The Pacific Championship** | "Super Rugby" is SANZAAR's |
| `jl1` | Japan League One | **Japan Elite League** | avoids the JRFU competition name |
| `cc` | Champions Cup | **European Elite Cup** | EPCR mark; "Elite" carries the same meaning |
| `chc` | European Challenge Cup | **The Continental Shield** | second-tier European trophy, new name |
| `sn` | Six Nations | **The European Nations Championship** | the most heavily enforced mark in the sport |
| `trc` | The Rugby Championship | **The Southern Nations Championship** | mirrors the northern one, as the real pair do |
| `wc` | Rugby World Cup | **The World Championship** | World Rugby enforce "Rugby World Cup" hard |
| `pnc` | Pacific Nations Cup | **The Islands Cup** | same region, different name |

## 2. Clubs and grounds

Club name becomes the city it plays in. Thirteen clubs share a city with another
club, so those take the locality their ground actually sits in - still
location-based, and unique. Ground names follow the naming shapes of their own
rugby culture rather than one global template.

## 3. Not changed

- **Player names.** ~1,600 real names stay exactly as they are, by decision.
- **Nations.** England, France, New Zealand and the rest are countries, not brands.
- **Club ids.** Internal only, never shown, and renaming them would break every save.
