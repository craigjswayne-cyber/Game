# Match simulation sources (rugby union, professional men's 15s)

Real-match reference figures for tuning the match engine. Every number below comes from a page that was opened. "Per match" means both teams added together; "per team" means one side in one match. "Derived" means simple division or addition of figures printed in the source, with the sum shown. Researched 24 Nov 2026.

Short names for sources used many times:

- **WR RWC2019**: World Rugby, Rugby World Cup 2019 Statistical Analysis (45 matches played). https://resources.world.rugby/worldrugby/document/2022/01/26/9106cf21-9a5f-4b99-b386-3f13eaeb6183/RWC_Analysis_2019-1-.pdf
- **WR 6N2017**: World Rugby, Six Nations 2017 Statistical Report (15 matches). https://resources.world.rugby/worldrugby/document/2022/01/26/16fb40d2-2ee9-464c-90cc-e74d52d99796/6_Nations_analysis_2017.pdf
- **WR RWC2023 MR5**: World Rugby, RWC 2023 pool-stage analysis of the eight quarter-finalists. https://resources.worldrugby-rims.pulselive.com/worldrugby/document/2023/10/10/9e295d99-9a35-4b2e-bd2e-09b668372075/RWC2023_Analysis_MR5_quarter-finalists_FINAL.pdf
- **SP EVO**: Stats Perform, "The Evolution of Rugby: A Statistical Analysis" (Rugby World Cups 1987 to 2023). https://www.statsperform.com/insights/the-evolution-of-rugby-a-statistical-analysis/
- **SP KICK**: Stats Perform, "Effectiveness of kicking game in rugby union" (Premiership 2014-15, 128 games). https://www.statsperform.com/resource/effectiveness-of-kicking-game-in-rugby-union/
- **NB1**: "The acute effects of home advantage on English professional rugby union", Northumbria Global Journal of Sport Science, Coaching and Medical Research (Premiership 2022-23, 119 matches). https://journals.northumbria.ac.uk/index.php/gjsscmr/article/download/1613/1856
- **ROE 2025**: Roe et al. (2025), "Spot the difference: contact event frequency during 30,000 women's and men's rugby union player matches", Stats Perform data, 2022-23. https://acuresearchbank.acu.edu.au/download/7a4bef06f7d61422d41669800b88016dbceff4dbbc6e9a03764a486d2cc74f74/1337542/OA_Roe_2025_Spot_the_difference_Contact_event_frequency.pdf
- **R365 URC5**: Rugby365, "Shape of the game: Bulls set the standard" (URC 2025-26 season review, URC data). https://rugby365.com/tournaments/united-rugby-championship/shape-of-the-game-bulls-set-the-standard/ (the same figures appear on RugbyPass: https://www.rugbypass.com/news/the-urc-stat-leinster-and-stormers-were-neck-to-neck-on/)
- **PR 6N2026**: Planet Rugby, "2026 Six Nations in numbers" (provider not named). https://www.planetrugby.com/news/2026-six-nations-in-numbers-penalty-kicks-deteriorate-as-most-attacking-championship-ever-shatters-records
- **OA 6N2026**: Opta Analyst, 2026 men's Six Nations stats review. https://theanalyst.com/articles/greatest-six-nations-ever-2026-mens-championship-stats-review
- **6N PREVIEW 2024**: Six Nations official preview report, England v Ireland (Opta data, 2024 Championship rounds 1 to 3). https://sixnationscorporate.ptsportsuite.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Preview-Report-England-v-Ireland-EN.pdf
- **ROBERTS 2024**: Roberts et al. (2024), "Injury in starting and replacement players from five professional men's rugby unions", Sports Medicine (Premiership, Welsh Pro14 sides, Super Rugby, 2016 to 2019, 1,224 matches). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11329520

## Disagreements worth knowing

- RWC 2019 points per match: World Rugby p.22 says 40, p.23 says 49 (pool 50, knockout 43). The 40 already in use comes from p.22.
- RWC 2019 lineout and scrum success: World Rugby says 88% and 93%; Stats Perform says 91% and 95%.
- Tackle success: Stats Perform World Cup 84% v Premiership study 88 to 89%.
- Turnovers won at RWC 2023: Ireland 6.7 (World Rugby article) v 5.3 (Reuters, World Rugby stats).
- Scrums per match: 13 to 15 (World Rugby, Stats Perform, Roe) v 10 to 11 (blog counting Six Nations scrums, likely without resets).

## What this means for the simulation

Targets are per match (both teams) unless marked per team. Tolerances are suggestions for a season-long average from many simulated games, not single matches.

- Points: 50 to 56 per match for a club-league feel (Premiership 2024-25 is 56.3); plus or minus 5.
- Tries: 6.5 to 8.0 per match (URC 2025-26 6.9, Premiership 2024-25 8.0); plus or minus 0.7.
- Possessions: about 85 per match, or about 42 to 50 per team; plus or minus 8.
- Phases per possession: mean about 2.7, with about 35% of possessions ending on the first phase and about 70 to 75% within three; plus or minus 0.4 on the mean.
- Ruck retention for the side in possession: 94%; plus or minus 1.5 points.
- Turnovers won: 6 to 8 per team; plus or minus 1.5.
- Tackles: 300 to 345 per match (World Cup 2023 about 338, URC 2025-26 344); plus or minus 30.
- Tackle success: 84 to 89%; aim for 87%, plus or minus 2 points.
- Line breaks: about 10 per match (5 per team); plus or minus 2.
- Defenders beaten: about 38 per match (19 per team); plus or minus 6.
- Offloads: 10 to 15 per match; plus or minus 4 (weak sources).
- Passes: 273 to 305 per match; plus or minus 25.
- Kicks from hand: about 46 per match; plus or minus 5.
- Scrums: 13 to 14 per match; plus or minus 2. Own-feed success 88 to 93%; plus or minus 3 points. Scrum penalties about 3.5 per match; plus or minus 1.
- Lineouts: 25 to 28 per match; plus or minus 3. Own-throw success 88 to 91%; plus or minus 3 points. Steals about 1 to 2 per team.
- Mauls: about 11 per match; plus or minus 3.
- Try sources: lineout about 45 to 50% of tries, scrum about 15%, kick returns about 15%, turnovers about 12%, restarts about 6%; plus or minus 5 points each.
- Tries from first phase (no ruck): about a third; plus or minus 6 points. Three or more rucks: about 44%.
- 22 entries: about 10 per team; plus or minus 2. Points per 22 entry: about 2.4 to 2.7 for an average side, 1.5 for weak and 3.5 for strong sides; plus or minus 0.4.
- Penalties: 17 to 22 per match (about 9 to 11 per team); plus or minus 3.
- Penalty options: about two thirds go to touch, the rest at goal.
- Goal kicking: conversions about 75%, penalties about 76 to 80%, overall about 72 to 75%; plus or minus 3 points. Close and central near 99%, beyond halfway near 38%, with distance mattering far more than angle.
- Drop goals: 0.05 to 0.15 per match; anything above 0.3 is too many.
- Yellow cards: 0.6 to 1.7 per match depending on era and competition; aim for about 1.0 to 1.5 for modern club rugby, plus or minus 0.4. Red cards: 0.1 to 0.2 per match.
- 50:22s: 0.4 to 0.5 per match; plus or minus 0.2.
- Contestable kicks regained: about 15 to 25%; plus or minus 5 points.
- Home advantage: home side wins 62 to 69%; aim for about 65%, plus or minus 4 points. Home margin about +6.5 points; plus or minus 2.
- Time-loss injuries: about 3 per match (1.5 per team), concussions about 0.75 per match; not all should force a replacement.
- Replacements: 7 or 8 used per team (about 15.5 per match); about 94% after half-time and about 60% in the last 25 minutes; forwards replaced far more than backs (replacements play about half of forwards' final-quarter minutes v about a quarter of backs').
- Knock-ons: at least 5 to 7 per match lead to scrums; use as a floor only (weak sources).
