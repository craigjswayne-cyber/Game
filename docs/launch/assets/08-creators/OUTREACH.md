# Creator and press outreach

Write like one developer contacting one person whose audience might enjoy the
game. Short. Specific to them. One ask. No "I hope this finds you well", no
"game-changing", no attachments larger than a screenshot. Send personally from
info@fwdsandbcks.com, never as a mail-merge blast.

Tracking: `creators.csv` (one row per person; fill as you go).

## Who

| Group | Why they might care | How many |
|---|---|---|
| Rugby YouTubers and podcasters | An audience that argues about selection every weekend | 8 to 10 |
| Football Manager / sports-management creators | They know the genre and like a new world to explore | 8 to 10 |
| Mobile strategy and sim reviewers | Phone-first, deep, offline | 5 to 8 |
| Rugby writers and sites | A story: one developer, a game that remembers | 5 |
| Ruck | News partner; first to know | 1 |

Research each one: watch two of their videos or read two of their pieces before
you write. Mention one by name. If you can't say why their audience would like
PHASE, don't send it.

## Review instructions (send when someone says yes)

```
Thanks for taking a look. A few pointers so PHASE shows you what it does:

1. Start a new career and pick a mid-table club. Lower expectations, more decisions.
2. Play at least three weeks. In the Press Room and in your office you'll be asked to promise things. Promise something you might not deliver.
3. After a defeat, read the Coach's Verdict at full time ("What hurt you most").
4. Keep an eye on News. When a promise is kept or broken, you'll hear about it, and so will agents.
5. If you have an hour, play a full season: the Annual, the Roll of Honour and the Manager Legacy start to fill in.

New to rugby? Manager menu > The Manager's Handbook explains every term.

Anything odd, email me directly: info@fwdsandbcks.com.
```

## Follow-up (once, after 7 days, then stop)

```
Hi [first name], just bumping this in case it got buried. No worries at all if it's not for you. [Your name]
```

## Rules

- One follow-up, never two.
- Never pay for coverage without it being disclosed; no affiliate codes at launch (launch programme section 11).
- Don't promise anything about the App Store date until it's real.
- Pro unlocks for reviewers: use Play Console promo codes (owner to set up), never a special build.
