## Descriptions

**One line.** PHASE: Rugby Manager is the rugby management game where the world remembers what you did.

**Short (about 50 words).** PHASE: Rugby Manager is a rugby union management game for phones in which every decision leaves a mark. Keep a promise and a player will run through a wall for you; break one and every agent hears about it. 171 clubs, the men's and women's game, six languages, fully offline.

**Long (about 150 words).** PHASE: Rugby Manager puts you in charge of a rugby union club and keeps a record of everything you do. Players remember the promises you kept and the ones you broke. Agents talk. Rival coaches carry their record against you from club to club, and a player you sold can line up against you. After every match the coach tells you exactly what hurt you and what to fix by Saturday. There are 171 clubs in 15 leagues across the men's and women's game, a transfer market with rival bids, an academy with its own league, a press room, a boardroom and a Manager Legacy that keeps every season on your record. It runs offline with no account, in English, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese and Afrikaans. PHASE is free to play on Google Play and coming to the App Store.

## Key features

- A consequence engine: promises, broken promises, dropped captains, released players and academy debuts are remembered and come back.
- Rivalries that are earned, and rival coaches whose record against you follows them.
- A Coach's Verdict after every match: what hurt you most, and two fixes checked the next week.
- Tactics: five attack and five defence styles, a set-piece playbook, moves opponents learn to read, the half-time talk.
- Transfers with rival bids and bidding wars, scouting that shows ranges, loans, contracts and a salary cap.
- An academy with its own league.
- The men's and women's game, 171 clubs, 15 leagues.
- Live text commentary with animated highlights of the big moments.
- Offline, no account, six languages.
