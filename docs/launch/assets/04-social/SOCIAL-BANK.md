# PHASE social bank

The PHASE account is a rugby publication that happens to make a game. Talk about
rugby, decisions, management, careers and stories. Sell rarely. Someone who
isn't ready to download should still want to follow.

Rules: British English. No em dashes. No exclamation marks. One idea per post.
At most one post in three asks anyone to download anything. Every game quote is
the engine's own text from the showcase career (or a player's career, shared
with their permission). Check `docs/launch/FACTS.md` before writing a new claim.

Graphics: `final/<ID>/` (made from `source/posts.json` by `scripts/launch/social.mjs`).
Hashtags: at most two, at the end, only where the platform rewards them
(Instagram, TikTok): `#rugby #rugbyunion`.
