The rugby management game where the world remembers what you did.

You take a job. You make the calls. And PHASE keeps the receipts.

Promise a youngster first-team rugby and keep your word, and he will run through a wall for you. Break it, and every agent in the game hears about it: the next player you want asks for more money up front. Drop your captain and the dressing room splits. Sell a favourite, and one day he lines up against you.

Every decision leaves a mark. Then there is another decision to make.

YOUR CLUB
• 171 clubs in 15 leagues: nine in the men's game, six in the women's, from the English top flight to Japan and the United States.
• A board with an opinion about you, a press pack that quotes you, and a town that remembers the derby longer than the table does.
• Facilities to build, sponsors to keep sweet, and books that have to balance.

YOUR PLAYERS
• Every player has form you can read, an injury history and a temperament you learn by watching.
• Promises, trust and morale that move with what you do. The dressing room tells you where you stand, from "They do not believe a word" to "They would run through a wall".
• An academy with its own league, mentors who bring the kids on, and debuts the club does not forget.

YOUR SQUAD
• A transfer market with agents who haggle, rivals who bid for your best players, and bidding wars you have to answer.
• Scouting that shows ranges until your scouts have seen a man play.
• Contracts, loans and a salary cap.

YOUR MATCHDAY
• Five attack styles, five defence styles, a set-piece playbook and drilled moves that opponents learn to read.
• Live text commentary and live stats, with animated highlights of the big moments.
• The half-time talk, the substitutions and the change of plan are yours. At full time the coach tells you exactly what hurt you, and gives you two things to fix by Saturday.

YOUR LEGACY
• Rivalries that are earned, not just scheduled, and rival coaches whose record against you follows them from club to club.
• A reputation built from what you actually do: "Known for giving young players their chance."
• The Annual, the Roll of Honour, a Hall of Fame and a Manager Legacy that keeps every season on your record.

NEW TO RUGBY?
The Manager's Handbook explains every number in the game and every word of the sport, in plain language, with a search box.

BUILT FOR A PHONE
• Portrait and one-handed, day and night modes, and a text-size setting.
• Plays offline: no account and no login. Your career is saved on your device after every week, with export and import so a backup is yours to keep.
• English, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese and Afrikaans.
• Free to play, with adverts. Optional "Free with ad" buttons are always your choice. Pro Manager removes the adverts and adds three skins; it changes nothing in the rugby.
