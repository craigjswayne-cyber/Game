# PHASE launch calendar

Day L is the public launch day, set by the owner once the release gate in
`LAUNCH-PROGRAMME.md` (section 15) is green. Everything is counted from it.

**One decision first:** PHASE is already in production on Google Play (code 36
was accepted on 29 Sep). So "launch" here means the public marketing launch of a
build that passes the gate, not the first day the app exists. That changes two
assets: "PHASE IS COMING" (LCH-01) only suits audiences who haven't seen it yet,
and "PHASE IS LIVE" (LCH-10) should say what is new. If the owner prefers, swap
LCH-01 for B1 and use LCH-10 as written. See the human actions list.

Rhythm: one post a day on Instagram, X and Facebook; three short videos a week on
TikTok, Reels and Shorts; Discord when there is news. At most one post in three
asks anyone to download. IDs refer to `assets/04-social/SOCIAL-BANK.md` (copy)
and `assets/04-social/final/` (graphics).
