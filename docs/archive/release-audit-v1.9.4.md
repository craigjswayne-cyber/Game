# PHASE: Rugby Manager 1.9.4: release audit (fixes ship as 1.9.5)

21 October 2026. Branch `qa/1.9.4-audit`, PR #70.

## A. Verdict: CONDITIONALLY READY, EXTERNAL CHECKS REQUIRED

The audit target was 1.9.4 at `a06cc76` (versionCode 63). That build was
uploaded to Play during the audit (owner, 21 Oct 2026), so every fix here
ships as **1.9.5, versionCode 64**.

1.9.4 as uploaded carries two P1 defects a player can meet: a watched
knockout tie that goes to tries or kicks can have its result changed
afterwards, and a manager who changes job takes his old board's agreed
building and staff fund with him. A third P1 (prototype pollution through a
hostile save import) needs a deliberately crafted file. None loses a save.
All three are fixed on the branch with regression tests.

On `42adf14` the Gate is green and the release check is green apart from
`aiecon`. 1.9.5 is ready to build; the device checks in section I come
before it is promoted past internal testing. The `aiecon` soak (section E)
fails on 1.9.4 and on 1.9.5 alike; it predates this audit and is reported
for a decision, not fixed.

**Condition (1.9.4).** The kick-off tank is untouched from 33.3%, 45 at
20%, continuous and never falling as condition rises. Every squad man stays
between 20 and 100 after every week, and saves keep each value exactly.
Playing left a man fresher than he started in 2 of 1,571 measured
appearances, both short stints off the bench, under 5 points: negligible.

**Balance note, not changed.** A replacement's tank never starts under 60
(`benchTank`, documented as deliberate), while a starter on 20% now kicks off
at 45. Below about 47% condition, starting a fresh man and sending a tired
star on in the first minute gives the star more legs. Small, and the 60
floor is a design decision.

**aiecon.** The fall in AI club income came in with the 1.9.2 merge
(`b0bb5b7`, which brought the 2026-27 squads and their wages, half-time
recovery, team talks and the HIA change), not with 1.9.4 or this audit's fixes. AI clubs stay solvent, the richest is
not printing money, and the worst-run is not absurd; the probe guards the
rate of gain. Section J has the options.

## F. Persistence and migration

Real saves were generated by the 1.6.5 (`0320553`), 1.8.3 (`fe6f12d`) and
1.9.3 (`b2a91f9`) source trees: men's at weeks 20, 30, 52 and 55, women's at
week 12. Each migrates deterministically, migrating twice equals once, five
round trips equal one, and players, contracts, condition, career rows,
fixtures, history, finances and facilities are kept; each then played six
weeks with every condition in range. 1.8.3 and 1.9.3 saves migrate byte for
byte the same as on `a06cc76`. Hostile and damaged inputs tested: truncated
JSON, wrong types, nulls, NaN and Infinity condition, duplicate and reserved
ids, unknown positions, extra fields, oversized files. Not tested: a real
device quota or eviction, and an OS kill mid-write.

## G. UI, accessibility and platform

844x390 landscape) by 35 screens, with no horizontal scroll, raw keys, literal

**Known, not fixed (owner's open item).** Tab-bar labels overlap on
Competitions, Nations, Dream Team and Handbook in all six languages, and on
Squad, Finances, Report, Tactics and Training at 320 wide. Cause:
`min-width: 44px` on `.tab-bar button` (`src/ui/styles/02-shell.css:583`, 18
Oct). Adding `flex-shrink: 0` to the same rule lays the strip out cleanly
and lets it scroll.

**Not fixed, P3 for the owner:** the active save's Delete is undone by the
next autosave (no data loss); the first-season checklist never expires; the
match-day picker still shows star ratings; shirt numbers use the system
monospace face; some images are not cached offline (predates 1.9.4).

Native: packaging checked by reading (versionCode from `version.json`,
versionName from `package.json`, Capacitor 8.5.3 and plugins consistent in
both shells, AdMob ids and permissions, ads SDK 25.4.0, fonts in the bundle).

## J. Remaining risks and recommendation

- **aiecon (open, needs a decision).** AI clubs gain about a third less a
  season than the probe's baseline since the 1.9.2 merge (`b0bb5b7`).
  Options: find which part of 1.9.2 did it and retune (a balance change), or
  record the new rate as the baseline with the reason. Not a blocker for
  1.9.5: every solvency check in the probe holds.
- **Tab bars.** A one-line CSS fix for a regression that now touches nine
  screens. Recommended for 1.9.5 or the next build.
- **Native.** The rewarded-ad fix and consent order are proved against
  faked plugins only. Section I covers them on a device.
- **Long careers.** Fix 7 changes how long careers play out (not the match
  stream); autopilotprobe and the econprobe panel hold.

Recommendation: merge PR #70 (done once the Gate passes on this report's
commit), build 1.9.5 on versionCode 64, upload to internal testing, and run
section I before promoting it.
