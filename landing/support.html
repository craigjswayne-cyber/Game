Support

How can we help.

Most answers are below. If yours isn't, email us or report it from inside the game, and a person will reply.

Contact

Email: info@fwdsandbcks.com. Tell us your phone, the game's version (Manager menu > About & legal) and what happened.

From the game: Manager menu > Report a Bug. It can attach the crash record, so we can see what went wrong.

Discord: the PHASE Discord, for bugs, ideas and talking rugby with other managers.

Questions

Purchases

I bought Pro Manager. How do I get it back after reinstalling or on a new phone?

Manager menu > About & legal > Support the game, then Restore a previous purchase. Use the same Google or Apple account you bought it with.

What does Pro Manager do?

It removes the adverts and adds three skins only Pro managers get. It changes nothing in the rugby: the game is the same game, free, either way.

A purchase says it is pending.

The store is still processing it. There is nothing more to do: it arrives on its own, and Restore picks it up if you close the game first.

I was charged but didn't get what I bought.

Try Restore first. If it still isn't there, email us with the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt. Refunds are handled by the store you paid through.

Saves and careers

Where is my career saved?

On your phone, after every week. There is no account and no cloud copy, so nothing about your career leaves your device unless you export it.

How do I move my career to a new phone, or back it up?

Manager menu > Save / Load Game > Export Career. Keep the file somewhere safe (email it to yourself, or put it in your cloud drive). On the new phone, open the same screen and choose Import Save File.

My career has gone.

Careers are stored by the app on the phone, so uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. If you exported a file, import it. If not, email us: we can't see your phone, but we'll help you check what is there.

Adverts and privacy

Can I change what I agreed to about adverts?

Yes, at any time: Manager menu > Settings > Advert privacy choices.

A "Free with ad" button didn't give me anything.

Sometimes no advert is available, or the connection drops before it finishes. Nothing is lost: try again later. The game itself never needs a connection.

What data does PHASE collect?

The game itself collects nothing and has no account. Adverts are served by Google AdMob, with your consent. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

The game

Do I need to be online?

No. The whole game runs on the phone. Only adverts use the network.

Is there multiplayer?

No. PHASE is a single-player career game.

Which languages does it support?

English, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese and Afrikaans. Change it in Settings.

I'm new to rugby. Where do I start?

Manager menu > How to play, then the Manager's Handbook, which explains every number in the game and every word of the sport, with a search box.

Is PHASE on iPhone?

Not yet. It is on Google Play now and coming to the App Store. Join the Discord to hear first.
