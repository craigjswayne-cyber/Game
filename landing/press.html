About the game

PHASE: Rugby Manager puts you in charge of a rugby union club and keeps a record of everything you do. Players remember the promises you kept and the ones you broke. Agents talk. Rival coaches carry their record against you from club to club, and a player you sold can line up against you. After every match the coach tells you exactly what hurt you and what to fix by Saturday.

There are 171 clubs in 15 leagues across the men's and women's game, a transfer market with rival bids, an academy with its own league, a press room, a boardroom and a Manager Legacy that keeps every season on your record. It runs offline with no account, in six languages.
