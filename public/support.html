Support

PHASE: Rugby Manager · last updated 10 October 2026

Contact

Email info@fwdsandbcks.com. A real person reads every message. Tell us your device, the version number (menu, About & legal) and what happened.

For a bug, the quickest route is Report a Bug in the game's menu. It attaches the details needed to fix it, and you send it yourself by email or copy and paste. Nothing is sent without you.

You can also talk to other managers and report problems on the PHASE Discord.

Purchases

I bought something and it has not arrived

Open the Store from the menu and press Restore a previous purchase. If the store was still processing the payment, it arrives on its own once the store confirms it, and Restore picks it up if you closed the game first.

I have a new phone, or reinstalled the game

Pro Manager is tied to your Google Play or Apple account, not to the phone. Sign in to the same account, open the Store and press Restore a previous purchase. Items used up inside a career, such as board funding, belong to that career and come back only with its save file.

Refunds

Payments are taken by Google Play or the App Store, so refunds are handled by them. On Android, use your order history in Google Play. On iPhone and iPad, use reportaproblem.apple.com. If you are stuck, email us and we will help where we can.

Adverts

The Android and iOS apps show a small banner on some screens and offer optional adverts in return for a reward. Pro Manager removes the banners on every device signed in to the account that bought it.

To change what you agreed to about adverts and data, open Settings, Advert privacy choices in the game (shown where the law requires it), or reset your advertising identifier in your phone's settings. The privacy policy has the detail.

Saved careers

Careers are saved on your device and nowhere else. We cannot see them or recover them. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes them.

To back up a career or move it to another device, open Save / Load. Send a copy hands the save file to your phone's share sheet, so you can put it in a cloud drive or email it to yourself. Export Career saves the file to the device. Either file goes back in through Import.

Something looks wrong on my screen

Try Settings first: text size, night mode and language are all there. If a screen still does not fit, send a screenshot with Report a Bug or by email, with your device name.
