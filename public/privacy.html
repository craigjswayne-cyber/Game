Privacy Policy

PHASE: Rugby Manager · last updated 5 September 2026

The short version: the game itself collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. The Android and iOS apps show a small number of adverts, served by Google AdMob, and that is the one place a third party is involved. Everything below is detail on those two sentences.

What is collected

By the game: nothing. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no telemetry of any kind. It does not ask for, and cannot use, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or notifications.

By the advertising in the apps: your device's advertising identifier and coarse technical data, as described under Advertising below. The web version at phaserugbymanager.com shows no adverts and collects nothing at all.

What is stored, and where

Your careers are saved on your own device, in the browser or app's private storage:

  • Saved careers - clubs, players, results, your whole history - in the device's local database (IndexedDB).
  • Settings - night mode, text size, language, and whether you have supported the game - in local storage.

None of it leaves the device. Nobody else can read it, including the developer. Deleting the app, or clearing the site data, deletes all of it permanently: there is no copy anywhere else, which is why the game has an Export button on the Save / Load screen for keeping your own backups.

Network use

The game itself is fully offline once loaded: the simulation makes no network requests, sends nothing anywhere, and its published source is checked automatically on every build for anything that could — network calls, third-party scripts, remote fonts — with the build failing if one appears. The one exception in the Android and iOS apps is advertising (below), which is the app shell's doing rather than the game's, and which the "Remove all ads" purchase switches off entirely.

Purchases

Where the game is distributed through an app store, it offers a small catalogue of optional one-off purchases (extra club funds, a squad recovery, facility upgrades and similar). Every transaction is handled entirely by the app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store) under their privacy policies. The game never sees your name, your payment details or your account: it is told only which purchases exist, and it records those ownership flags on your device. What a purchase adds inside the game is written on the product itself; none of them collect or transmit anything, and none of them play the matches for you.

Advertising

The Android and iOS apps show a small number of banner adverts at the foot of two screens, and offer a few optional "watch an advert instead" buttons that replace a fee the game would otherwise charge. Adverts are served by Google AdMob. Advertising is the only thing in the apps that uses the network and the only thing that involves a third party. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier and coarse technical data (device model, operating system version, rough region derived from your IP address) to serve and measure adverts, under Google's privacy policy; the partners it may share data with are listed on Google's partner page.

Where the law requires it you are asked for consent before the first advert, and on iOS you are asked whether the app may track you. Saying no to either limits adverts to non-personalised ones; it never limits the game. Adverts never appear on the title screen, during a match or over a dialogue, and none of the "watch an advert" buttons gives a paying player anything a non-paying one cannot earn.

A one-off "Remove all ads" purchase removes every banner permanently; the optional "watch an advert" buttons remain, because you are the one asking for them. You can also reset or delete your device's advertising identifier at any time in your phone's settings. The web version of the game shows no advertising at all.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anybody. Adverts in the apps are limited to content rated for general audiences, and the game is not directed at children. It contains no chat, no user-generated content and no social features.

Deleting your data

PHASE: Rugby Manager holds no account and no data about you anywhere but on your own phone, so there is nothing to ask us to delete. To delete everything the app has stored:

  • Your careers and settings are on your device only. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes all of them at once and permanently. If you want to keep a career first, export it from the Save / Load screen.
  • The advertising identifier that Google AdMob may use in the apps belongs to your phone, not to us. Reset or delete it in your phone's settings: on Android, Settings → Privacy → Ads; on iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to withdraw permission. Google's handling of it is described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Purchases are records held by Google Play or the Apple App Store under your account with them, not by us.

Nothing is kept by the developer, so there is no retention period: once it is gone from your device, it is gone. If you have a question about any of this, write to the address below.

Your rights

The game holds nothing about you to access, correct, export or erase. The data on your device is yours and under your control: export it from the Save / Load screen, or delete it by clearing the app's data. For data handled by Google AdMob, Google's privacy policy explains your choices, and your phone's settings let you reset or delete the advertising identifier it uses.

A note on names
