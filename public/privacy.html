Privacy Policy

PHASE: Rugby Manager · last updated 23 August 2026

The short version: this game collects nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere. Everything below is detail on that one sentence.

What is collected

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting service and no telemetry of any kind. It does not ask for, and cannot use, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or notifications.

What is stored, and where

Your careers are saved on your own device, in the browser or app's private storage:

  • Saved careers - clubs, players, results, your whole history - in the device's local database (IndexedDB).
  • Settings - night mode, text size, language, and whether you have supported the game - in local storage.

None of it leaves the device. Nobody else can read it, including the developer. Deleting the app, or clearing the site data, deletes all of it permanently: there is no copy anywhere else, which is why the game has an Export button on the Save / Load screen for keeping your own backups.

Network use

The game is fully offline once loaded. Beyond downloading the app itself from its host, and any update, it makes no network requests at all. The published source is checked automatically on every build for anything that could send data — network calls, third-party scripts, remote fonts — and the build fails if one appears.

Purchases

Where the game is distributed through an app store, it offers a small catalogue of optional one-off purchases (extra club funds, a squad recovery, facility upgrades and similar). Every transaction is handled entirely by the app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store) under their privacy policies. The game never sees your name, your payment details or your account: it is told only which purchases exist, and it records those ownership flags on your device. What a purchase adds inside the game is written on the product itself; none of them collect or transmit anything, and none of them play the matches for you.

Advertising

This build shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDK. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before it ships, and it will name the network involved and what it collects.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, collects no personal data from anybody, and therefore collects none from children. It contains no chat, no user-generated content and no social features.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing held about you to access, correct, export or erase. The data on your device is yours and under your control: export it from the Save / Load screen, or delete it by clearing the app's data.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or anything else: phaserugbymanager@gmail.com.

A note on names

PHASE: Rugby Manager is an unofficial, independent game. Clubs, competitions, grounds and sponsors in it are fictional. Player names are real, used for identification in a sporting database and nothing else; the game is not affiliated with, endorsed by, licensed by or connected to any player, club, league or governing body. No official badges, kits or logos are included. If you are a player named in the game and would rather not be, write to the address above and you will be removed in the next update.

This policy applies to the web version at its published address and to any app-store build made from the same source.
