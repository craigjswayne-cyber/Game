About us

We are the developers of PHASE: Rugby Manager. We build games that respect the player's time: they work offline, ask for no account and collect no personal data.

The studio is based in Covent Garden, London.

PHASE: Rugby Manager

A rugby union management game. Take a club from the bottom to the top across more than a hundred clubs and eight competitions, with transfers, contracts, the press and live match days.

  • Free to play on Android, coming soon to iPhone
  • Plays fully offline, with no account
  • Available in six languages
